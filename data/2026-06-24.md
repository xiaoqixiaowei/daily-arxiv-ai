### [20] [Neuro-Symbolic Drive: Rule-Grounded Faithful Reasoning for Driving VLAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23938)
*Xiangbo Gao,Xiukun Huang,Boyu Lu,Junge Zhang,Mengjie Mao,Jiachen Li,Wei Xiong,Zhengzhong Tu*

第一机构: 德州农工

Main category: cs.CL

TL;DR: 本文提出了Neuro-Symbolic Drive框架，通过从经典基于规则的规划器中提取规则推理轨迹来监督驾驶VLA模型，实现了推理与运动的因果关联，显著降低了轨迹预测误差。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的驾驶VLA模型虽然采用思维链推理，但其推理过程往往缺乏必要的决策语义，导致推理逻辑与规划运动之间存在因果断连，无法保证理由对行为的可信解释性。

Method: 提出一种神经符号框架，在仿真环境中对基于规则的规划器进行改造，提取其内部决策过程并序列化为结构化的规则推理轨迹，利用这些轨迹微调Qwen3.5-4B模型，从而将规划器的符号逻辑转化为对VLA的结构化监督。

Result: 在模拟器生成的基准测试中，该方法显著降低了预测误差。在三摄像头设定下，ADE@3s从0.47降至0.26，Miss Rate从8.30%降至6.40%；在八摄像头设定下，ADE@3s从0.54降至0.26，Miss Rate从10.13%降至5.99%。

Conclusion: Neuro-Symbolic Drive成功将神经符号规划逻辑转化为结构化监督信号，通过构建推理与运动生成的结构性耦合，实现了更忠实、更准确的驾驶推理与规划。

Abstract: Driving VLA models incorporating Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning are attractive because they leverage pretrained VLM representations and expose intermediate decisions in natural language, yet current rationales often lack the step-by-step decision semantics needed to keep the rationale causally connected to the planned motion. We introduce Neuro-Symbolic Drive, a neuro-symbolic driving framework that supervises a driving VLA with rule-grounded reasoning traces extracted directly from classical rule-based planners. Our key observation is that rule-based planners are symbolic AI systems that already function as executable reasoning engines: they reason about active safety constraints, search over candidate maneuvers, and select a final trajectory. We instrument these planners in simulation to capture both the executed trajectory and the internal decision trace at each rule-evaluation step. Each trace is serialized into structured rule-grounded reasoning and paired with the trajectory to fine-tune Qwen3.5-4B as a driving VLA. Because these traces are derived directly from the planner states that determine the action, they ensure reasoning is structurally coupled to motion generation by construction, rather than by post-hoc alignment. On our simulator-generated benchmark, detailed rule-grounded reasoning reduces ADE@3s from 0.47 to 0.26 and miss rate from 8.30% to 6.40% under three-camera perception, and from 0.54 to 0.26 and 10.13% to 5.99% under eight-camera perception. Neuro-Symbolic Drive thus converts neuro-symbolic planning logic into structured supervision. Code base: this https URL .

### [21] [AutoSpec: Safety Rule Evolution for LLM Agents via Inductive Logic Programming](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24245)
*Pingchuan Ma,Zhaoyu Wang,Zimo Ji,Yuguang Zhou,Zhantong Xue,Zongjie Li,Shuai Wang,Xiaoqin Zhang*

第一机构: 香港科技大学

Main category: cs.SE

TL;DR: 提出AutoSpec框架，通过归纳逻辑编程自动演化LLM代理的安全规则，平衡可解释性与精确率/召回率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 手工安全规则难以平衡误报和漏报，神经网络缺乏可解释性，现有方法无法同时满足安全性和可审计性。

Method: 基于反例引导的归纳综合（CEGIS）和归纳逻辑编程（ILP），从专家规则和标注轨迹出发，迭代挖掘误报/漏报反例，学习判别谓词并生成候选规则编辑，最终选出最优修订。

Result: 在代码执行和具身代理两个领域的291条轨迹上，规则F1分别达到0.98和0.93，误报率降低高达94%，在4-5次迭代内收敛，比启发式CEGIS方法F1提升最多4.8倍。

Conclusion: AutoSpec生成的规则可读、可审计，且能泛化到未见场景，为LLM代理的安全部署提供了有效方案。

Abstract: Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly automate complex tasks by integrating language models with external tools and environments. However, their autonomy poses significant safety risks: agents may execute destructive commands, leak sensitive data, or violate domain constraints. Existing safety approaches face a fundamental tradeoff: hand-crafted rules are interpretable but brittle, with overly conservative rules blocking safe operations (high false positives) while permissive rules miss unsafe behaviors (high false negatives). Neural classifiers lack the interpretability required for safety-critical deployments. We present AutoSpec, a framework that automatically evolves deployed expert-designed safety rules from user safe/unsafe annotations through counterexample-guided inductive synthesis (CEGIS) guided by inductive logic programming (ILP). Starting from the expert rules and a stream of annotated traces, AutoSpec iteratively evaluates rules, mines false-positive and false-negative counterexamples, uses ILP to learn which predicates discriminate them, generates candidate rule edits, and verifies candidates to select the best revision. The key insight is that ILP efficiently identifies predicates that appear frequently in false negatives but rarely in false positives (or vice versa), dramatically pruning the exponential search space of rule edits. This continues until convergence, producing interpretable rules that balance precision and recall. We evaluate AutoSpec on 291 execution traces spanning code execution and embodied agent domains. AutoSpec raises rule F1 to 0.98 and 0.93 across the two domains, achieving up to 94% false positive reduction while maintaining high recall, and converges within 4-5 iterations. The ILP-guided approach achieves up to 4.8x higher F1 than heuristic CEGIS. The learned rules are human-readable, auditable, and generalize to unseen scenarios.
